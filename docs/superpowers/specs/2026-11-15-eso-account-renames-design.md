# ESO account renames — approved design

Admins and Users may rename a member from member history; Viewers may not. Preview affected records and conflicts before a confirmed change. Give members a permanent GuildSync identity and retain all old names as searchable aliases. Preserve roster/rank/history/notes, banking, applications and confirmed Discord links. Record old/new names, authenticated operator and timestamp as a permanent member-history event. Old SavedVariables names must resolve to the renamed identity. Never silently merge another account, rewrite free-text content, grant roles, or synthesize a join. Conflicting destination records require manual review; automatic merge is out of scope. Original structured account names remain available for historical display. Existing historical spreadsheet files are not edited.

Considered roster-only edit (breaks history), global replacement (old imports recreate account), and permanent identity with aliases (chosen). Integrate canonical working keys into existing SQL while preserving original structured names and stable identity in dedicated tables. Rename and ingestion serialize on a database-scoped lock. Confirmation is short-lived and bound to authenticated user/session and preview; recheck under lock. Startup performs idempotent schema setup and identity backfill. User approved workflow and Admin/User permissions in conversation.
