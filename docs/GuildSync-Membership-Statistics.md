# Membership highlight categories

The last two Community highlights cards show **ESO guild: most recorded joins**
and **Discord server: most recorded joins**. These are separate membership
sources; one measures joining the ESO guild and the other joining Discord.
Both count recorded joins, including genuine rejoins. No leave rankings are
displayed. Existing join and departure history remains stored unchanged.

ESO counts come from roster join events. Discord counts come from live server
join history, excluding bulk snapshots. First discovery during a live update
may appear as a join, and retained history may be incomplete.

Deploy and restart the backend, then refresh Community highlights. This change
does not require a client build, Discord bot update, or Google Apps Script update.
